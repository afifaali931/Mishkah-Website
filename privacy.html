Mishkah Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 August 2026 · Applies to the Mishkah Android app (package com.mishkah.quran)

The short version: Mishkah has no server, no account, no ads, and no analytics. It does not even request internet access. Everything the app stores — your bookmarks, your memorization progress, your reading streak — stays on your device, and nothing is ever transmitted anywhere.

What Mishkah accesses

Mishkah is built to work fully offline. It does not collect, transmit, or receive any data over the internet, because it does not request network access at all. The only device data it reads is listed below, and every use is local to your device.

DataWhyLeaves the device?
Location (coarse or precise)Computes the Qibla bearing and today's prayer times for where you areNever
Notification deliveryShows the prayer-time reminder you opted intoNever — it's a local, on-device notification
Bookmarks & memorization progressLets you save ayat and track hifz progressNever — stored in a local database only

Where your data is stored

Bookmarks, memorization progress, your reading streak, and your last-computed location are stored in a local database and local preferences store on your device, using standard Android storage (Room and DataStore). None of it is backed up to any server Mishkah operates, because Mishkah doesn't operate one.

Sharing & third parties

Mishkah contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties — there is nothing to share, since nothing leaves your device. The Quran text and translations bundled in the app are static, read-only content shipped inside the app itself, not fetched from any server.

Permissions, explained

  • Location — used only inside the Qibla tab, to calculate the direction to the Kaaba and your local prayer times. You can deny this and still use every other part of the app; the Qibla compass and prayer times simply won't be available.
  • Notifications — used only to show a reminder at prayer time, if you turn that toggle on. You can deny or later revoke this in your device's notification settings at any time.
  • Exact alarm & boot — used only to make sure your prayer-time reminder fires at the correct minute and keeps working after you restart your phone. It schedules no other alarms and runs no other background task.

Children's privacy

Mishkah is a general-audience reading app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — it doesn't collect personal information from anyone at all, by design, as explained above.

Your choices

Because everything Mishkah stores lives only on your device, you're always in full control of it:

  • Revoke location or notification permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Mishkah → Permissions.
  • Clear your bookmarks and memorization progress from within the app, or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything Mishkah ever stored, since none of it exists anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of Mishkah adds a feature that does use the network — the "Effective" date at the top of this page will be updated, and the change will be described in the app's Play Store release notes.
