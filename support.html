Frequently asked questions

Does Mishkah need internet access?

No. The full Quran, both translations, and all app features work completely offline — Mishkah doesn't request internet permission at all. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I bookmark or memorize an ayah?

Open any surah from the Chapters tab, then use the ★ button on a verse to bookmark it, or the memorize action to mark it as memorized. Bookmarked ayat appear under the Bookmarks tab; memorized ones feed your progress on the Hifz tab.

Why is my Qibla compass inaccurate or jumpy?

Phone compasses drift over time and need periodic calibration — move your phone in a slow figure-8 motion for a few seconds. Also keep it away from metal objects, magnets, and speakers, which throw off the magnetic sensor.

I turned on prayer reminders but got no notification.

Check three things: notification permission is granted (Settings → Apps → Mishkah → Notifications), the Qibla tab has a location fix (reminders need to know where you are), and your device's battery optimization isn't restricting Mishkah in the background — some phones aggressively kill scheduled alarms unless the app is excluded from battery optimization.

How do I see a translation of a verse?

Tap the EN or اردو button on any ayah to reveal the English (Saheeh International) or Urdu (Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry) translation. Each toggles independently per verse.

Do I need an account?

No — there's no sign-in anywhere in the app. Everything you save lives locally on your device.

Will my bookmarks and progress survive a phone change?

Not automatically today — bookmarks and hifz progress are stored only on your device and aren't backed up to any account. Keep that in mind before uninstalling or switching phones.
