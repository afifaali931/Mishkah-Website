Inside the app

Everything for reading, memorizing,
and praying — in one place.

114 surahs, 6,236 ayat, authentic Uthmani script — and the tools built around them.

The complete Quran

All 114 surahs in Uthmani script, set in the Amiri Quran typeface, with English and Urdu translation on tap.

Hifz tracker

Mark ayat as memorized and watch a live dashboard: percent complete, surahs finished, and a daily streak.

Live Qibla compass

Real-time bearing to the Kaaba, corrected for magnetic declination, with turn-by-turn guidance.

Prayer reminders

Accurate prayer times for your location, with a gentle notification at Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.

Bookmarks & share

Save any ayah with a tap, then share it as beautifully formatted text.

Instant search

Find any surah by Arabic name, English name, or number in an instant.

Built different

Private by design, not by policy.

Mishkah doesn't even ask for internet access. The Quran, both translations, and every bookmark you save stay on your device — there's no server to send them to.

  • No ads, ever
  • No analytics or trackers
  • No account or sign-in
  • Works fully offline
